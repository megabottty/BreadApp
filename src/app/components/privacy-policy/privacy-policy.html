Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 17, 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect account, tenant, order, and communication data needed to operate BreadApp Business, including profile details, order content, and operational settings.

2. How We Use Data

Data is used to provide storefront, order management, billing, and notifications features. We use this information to run the service, improve reliability, and support customers.

3. Payment Data

Card payments are processed by Stripe. BreadApp Business does not store full payment card numbers. Payment metadata may be retained for order reconciliation and support.

4. Message Delivery

SMS and email notifications are delivered through third-party providers (including Twilio and SMTP services) based on tenant configuration and customer preferences.

5. Tenant Data Isolation

BreadApp Business is designed for tenant isolation. Access controls and data policies are intended to keep one business from accessing another business's data.

6. Security and Retention

We apply security controls to protect data in transit and at rest. Data retention varies by feature and legal obligation. Tenants are responsible for compliance requirements in their jurisdictions.

7. Contact

For privacy questions, use the Contact Us form in the storefront footer.
