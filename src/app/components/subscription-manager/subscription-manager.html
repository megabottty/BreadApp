How subscriptions work

🥖

Baked for your day

Subscription loaves are baked fresh the morning of your Monday or Tuesday pickup.

💬

Thursday check-in

Each Thursday we text or email you. Reply YES to confirm or SKIP to skip that week. No reply means we bake as usual.

📅

Skip or pause anytime

Skipping a week credits that week's price on your next payment. Pausing stops all future weeks until you resume.

🔔

Your notifications

Choose text or email for check-ins, order updates and promotions on your notification settings.
