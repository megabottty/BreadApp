🥣 Feeding Instructions

To keep your starter healthy and active, follow this standard feeding ratio:

  • 1 part Starter (by weight)
  • 1 part Flour (unbleached all-purpose or bread flour)
  • 1 part Water (room temperature, filtered if possible)

Example: 50g starter + 50g flour + 50g water.

🏠 Storage Options

On the Counter (Baking Daily)

Feed every 12-24 hours. Keep at room temperature. Do not let it get above 85°F.

In the Fridge (Baking Weekly)

Feed once a week. Take it out 4-8 hours before you plan to bake to "wake it up" with a feeding.

⚠️ Important Tips

  • Never use all of it! Always save at least a small amount (even just a tablespoon) to feed and regrow.
  • Avoid metal: Glass or plastic containers are best for long-term storage.
  • Hooches happen: If a dark liquid forms on top, don't worry! It's just alcohol/acid telling you it's hungry. Either pour it off or stir it back in and feed immediately.

🍞 When is it ready to bake?

A healthy starter should double in size within 4-8 hours after feeding. You can also try the "Float Test": drop a small spoonful of active starter into water. If it floats, it's ready to bake!
